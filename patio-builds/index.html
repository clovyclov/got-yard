Frequently Asked Questions

Dream Patio Build FAQs in Snohomish County, WA

Got questions about landscape permits, drainage, or project timelines in Snohomish County? Here are answers to common questions from local homeowners.

What types of patio builds does Got Yard design and construct? +

We design and build complete outdoor living spaces, including interlocking stone paver patios, natural flagstone patios, covered patio rooms, pergolas, built-in fire pits and fireplaces, outdoor kitchens, seating walls, and patio-to-deck seamless combinations.

How much does a custom dream patio build cost in Snohomish County? +

Complete patio build costs depend on overall square footage, material selections, grade adjustments, and custom additions like fire tables, seating walls, or covered structures. Standard hardscape patio builds range from $18 to $35 per sq ft, while multi-element outdoor living builds vary based on scope. Got Yard provides 100% free, itemized in-person quotes!

Can you combine a patio build with a composite deck or covered pergola? +

Yes! Multi-level outdoor living builds combining a stone paver patio ground floor with a elevated composite deck, outdoor fireplace, or timber pergola are one of our core specialties. We design seamless transitions between your home back door, deck, and patio area.

How do you prevent patio settlement and drainage issues in wet Washington weather? +

Every Got Yard patio build is engineered over a heavy-duty 6-inch compacted crushed gravel base with geotextile soil stabilization. We install integrated French drain systems, catch basins, and pitch the patio surface to channel heavy Northwest rainwater safely away from your home foundation.

How long does a full patio build project take from start to finish? +

Most custom patio builds take 3 to 7 business days from site excavation to final polymeric joint sealing and site clean-up. Larger multi-element builds featuring pergolas, retaining walls, or outdoor kitchens typically range from 1 to 2 weeks.
